Privacy policy

The app stays on your Mac

Moteplay is developed by YEONWOO KIM. We do not collect or transmit personal or usage data through the app. Moteplay has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, developer backend, or network connection for its core functions. No third-party SDK is bundled.

Scenes, names you give them and settings are stored locally in the app's Application Support container. They are not sent to the developer and are not automatically synchronized through iCloud. Moteplay does not read other windows, global keystrokes, contacts, location or screen recordings. It does not request screen-recording or accessibility permission.

Files, retention and deletion

A PNG export writes the scene image only to a location you choose in the system Save panel. External folders and file providers you choose have their own privacy and retention practices. Exported images stay where you save them until you delete them.

Saved scenes remain on this Mac until you remove them. Deleting a saved copy requires confirmation and does not delete the current playground. An unreadable save is kept untouched; choosing a fresh start preserves the unreadable original separately. To remove all app data, quit Moteplay and remove its local container (bundle identifier com.ongsim.moteplay), or contact support for guidance. Uninstalling the app alone may leave local data behind. No server-side record or account exists for us to delete.

Optional external services

Apple manages the paid download and any App Store diagnostics a user elects to share, under Apple's policies. Moteplay has no in-app purchase or subscription.

Opening support or this policy uses your browser. This static site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement. The site adds no analytics, advertisements or tracking scripts.

If you email support, the information you choose to send and your email address are handled by the email providers and used to respond to your request. We do not ask for passwords or private scene files. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve and document the request; contact us to request deletion.

Contact and changes

Questions or deletion requests: ppupy1200@gmail.com. Changes to this policy will be posted here with a revised date. New collection would require an updated disclosure and any applicable consent.

Moteplay 앱은 개인 정보와 사용 정보를 수집하거나 전송하지 않습니다. 계정, 광고, 사용 분석, 추적, 개발자 서버, 외부 SDK가 없습니다. 놀이와 저장은 인터넷 없이 작동합니다. 다른 창, 전역 키 입력, 연락처, 위치, 화면 녹화 내용을 읽지 않으며 화면 녹화와 손쉬운 사용 권한을 요구하지 않습니다.

Apple은 유료 다운로드와 사용자가 선택해 공유한 App Store 진단을 자체 방침에 따라 처리합니다. 앱 내 구매와 구독은 없습니다. 지원 사이트와 정책 링크는 브라우저에서 열립니다. GitHub Pages는 일반적인 웹 요청 정보를 처리할 수 있으나 이 사이트는 별도 분석·광고·추적 스크립트를 넣지 않습니다.
